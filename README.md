Open `index.html` in a browser. This version follows the supplied desktop screenshots and is responsive for tablet and mobile.

## Replacing project images

All replaceable images are in:

`assets/projects/`

Keep the same filenames when replacing images, or update the matching `src` value in `index.html`.

Recommended replacement sizes:

- `portrait.jpg` and `about-portrait.jpg`: portrait image, at least 900 × 1100 px
- `nike-red.jpg`, `donut.jpg`, `parallax.jpg`: wide image, at least 1600 × 800 px
- `bewaz.jpg`: 1400 × 950 px
- `gilded-table.jpg`: 1000 × 600 px
- `apple.jpg`: 1600 × 900 px
- `bloomscape.jpg`: 900 × 700 px
- `tokyo-drift.jpg`: 1200 × 800 px
- Service thumbnails: at least 800 × 650 px

The current image files are temporary crops from your screenshots. Replace them with your original high-resolution project images as you upload them.
